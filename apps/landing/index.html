Sanctuary Farm

A farm to care for. Math that quietly gets easier.

Kids feed the animals, hatch eggs, and tend the paddock. Every action is powered by a math answer they type — no multiple choice, no guessing. The game gets harder only as they get faster, and it says goodnight on time.

Why it exists

I built this for my 10-year-old, who needed multiplication practice and hated worksheets. The idea is simple: make the math the engine of a game worth playing, reward speed and not just correctness (that's what fluency actually is), and never punish a wrong answer — the animals just get a little glum, never hungry or gone.

It should reach any kid. That means a link that works on a Chromebook, an old iPad, or a library PC — nothing to install, nothing to buy, nothing to sign up for. If a teacher wants to point a class at it, that should just work.

Math advances, exploration reveals

New areas of the farm open because the math moved forward, not because time passed.

Latency drives mastery

A fact counts as learned when it's fast and correct, several times, spaced out.

Sessions end well

The game wraps up and says goodnight. It never nags you to come back.

No data collection

No name, age, email, location, or photos — ever. Saved progress uses a code or a made-up username, nothing real.
